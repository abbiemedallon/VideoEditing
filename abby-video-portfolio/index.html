WHAT I EDIT

Three things I do every week — and the reason each one works.

Short-Form Ads & UGC

Scroll-stopping 15–60s creatives for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Hook in the first 2 seconds, pattern interrupts to hold retention, kinetic captions, SFX and B-roll cut to the script. I also build multiple variations of a winning ad so you can keep testing angles, hooks and CTAs without starting from zero.

AI Video & Voiceover

AI-generated footage and avatars produced with Veo/Google Flow, paired with natural ElevenLabs voiceover and cut into finished ads. Useful when there's no creator footage yet, when you need a spokesperson on demand, or when you want to test a concept before paying for a shoot. Includes AI UGC and VSL-style creatives.

Long-Form — 3 to 6 Minutes

YouTube videos, VSLs, product explainers and event pieces in the 3–6 minute range. Story structure that keeps people watching, clean audio and music beds, colour-corrected footage, motion graphics and lower-thirds. Every long-form video can also be cut down into Shorts and Reels from the same session.

HOW I EDIT

The same five steps, whether it's a 20-second ad or a 6-minute video.

1

Script & Hook

Read the script or brief first, then decide what the opening 2 seconds have to do.

2

Footage & B-Roll

Pull creator, product, stock or AI-generated footage and match every shot to a line of VO.

3

Rough Cut

Build the structure — hook, body, product, proof, CTA — and get the pacing right before polish.

4

Polish

Captions, graphics, SFX, transitions, colour and audio levelling. No dead air, no drift.

5

Deliver & Iterate

Send, revise fast, then build variations of whatever performs.
