ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Abby — a video editor working across three formats: short-form ads and UGC creatives, AI-generated video with voiceover, and long-form 3–6 minute edits. Most of my week is spent turning scripts and raw footage into cuts that people actually finish watching.

On the short-form side I edit for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok — testing hooks, cutting for retention, matching B-roll to voiceover, and building variations of whatever performs so a winning ad can keep running. I work mainly in CapCut and Premiere Pro, and I'm comfortable with creator footage, product footage, raw phone clips and AI-generated material.

On the AI side I use Veo / Google Flow to generate footage and avatars and ElevenLabs for voiceover, which lets me build a full creative when there's no shoot budget or when a concept needs testing quickly. I've made AI UGC videos, specialist-character spokespersons, and VSL-style ads this way.

Long-form is where I started. I edited wedding films at Cinemanahan before moving into ads, so pacing, music, colour and audio in a 3–6 minute edit are familiar ground — and every long-form video I cut can be sliced into Shorts and Reels from the same session.

I also design graphics and manage social media, which means I can handle the thumbnails, covers and post copy around a video instead of handing back just the file.
